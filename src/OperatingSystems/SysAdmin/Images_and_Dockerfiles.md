# Configuring our images with Dockerfiles

So now we know how to run docker containers from an image we have pulled from the internet and also how to administer it, but and that is a big butt, the power of containers don't stay there, it comes in the form of Dockerfiles.

As you may imagine dockerfiles is a way to declaratively configure a personalized docker image with everything we might need for well a container image
